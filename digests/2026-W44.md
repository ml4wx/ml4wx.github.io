# 🌦️ ML4WX weekly digest · 2026-W44

**41 new papers** · **2 highlights** · 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-26

This week's papers cluster around data assimilation and observations, with several also questioning how ML skill is measured. The two highlights are the most useful reads: NNJA, a curated 1979-to-present observation archive with an AI-ready Parquet version for reanalysis and model development, and Batched NN 4D-Var, which shows that batched online learning of model-error corrections in ECMWF's IFS improves out-of-sample hybrid forecasts where sequential training did not generalize. Two papers on evaluation are also worth your time. "A Simple Climatology Benchmark Outperforms Machine-Learning Lightning Parameterizations" finds that a training-mean climatology explains 92% of daily lightning variance, and "Wet-Biased Data Pipelines Mislead the Training and Evaluation of Generative Precipitation Nowcasting" shows that wet-biased test sampling can reverse calibration conclusions. Several other papers this week are theoretical, or report no validation or quantitative results.

## ⭐ Highlights

**[NNJA—a curated archive of Earth system observations from 1970s to present for reanalysis and data-driven model development](https://doi.org/10.1175/bams-d-25-0157.1)** · NASA, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), NSF NCAR / UCAR, Brightband · Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society
Curated AI-ready archive of 1979-present observations in Parquet; a valuable community resource for observation-based reanalysis and data-driven model training.

**[Batched NN 4D-Var: a performant hybrid model framework for the Integrated Forecasting System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32582)** · ECMWF
Batched online NN 4D-Var in ECMWF IFS gives hybrid model-error corrections that generalize out of sample, a meaningful step for hybrid ML-NWP.


## Nowcasting (3)

- **[Physics-Informed Neural Network Emulation of Convective Storm Initiation over the Sahel: Calibration Bounds and Warning-Lead-Time Equity for Climate Analytics](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23545314)**
  Proposes a physics-informed neural network for Sahel convective storm initiation with calibration and lead-time equity bounds, illustrated on one Kampala temperature record; largely theoretical.
- **[Investigating the Use of Geostationary Satellites to Identify Thunderstorm Initiation with the LightningCast and ThunderCast Machine Learning Models](https://doi.org/10.1175/waf-d-25-0250.1)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Case-study evaluation of LightningCast and ThunderCast shows satellite-based nowcasts can falsely flag non-lightning convective storms with glaciation signatures as developing thunderstorms.
- **[Wet-Biased Data Pipelines Mislead the Training and Evaluation of Generative Precipitation Nowcasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26486)** · Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD)
  Shows wet-biased test sampling can reverse calibration conclusions for a satellite-to-radar diffusion nowcasting model, and that matched training and test sampling hides training bias.

## Global medium-range NWP (2)

- **[GraphCast-Mamba: Learning Non-Markovian Corrections for Weather Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28512)** · Google Research, Princeton University
  Adds a trainable Mamba and graph correction branch to a frozen GraphCast, cutting ten-day forecast MSE by 14.4% and day-10 MSE by 21.7%.
- **[UtoMe: Observation-Uncertainty-Guided Token Merging for Weather Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25001)** · ECMWF, Oak Ridge National Laboratory
  Uncertainty-guided token merging for weather transformers cuts ERA5 72-hour Overall error from 67.32 to 53.00 at 50% token reduction with similar FLOPs.

## Regional & km-scale models (1)

- **[Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Network Emulation of Regional Climate Models at Convection-Permitting Resolution: Spectral Fidelity Bounds and Downstream Flood-Exposure Equity Across West African River Basins](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23545305)**
  Theoretical spectral fidelity bounds for graph neural network emulators of convection-permitting regional climate models, linking spectral bias to flood-exposure inequity in West African basins; no training.

## Local & station forecasting (3)

- **[INTELLIGENT WEATHER PREDICTION SYSTEM USING MACHINE LEARNING AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23586598)**
  Draft system combining SVM, KNN and LSTM with a user interface for weather prediction; no quantitative results are reported.
- **[Probabilistic Machine Learning for Short-Term Atmospheric Seeing Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29654)**
  Benchmarks probabilistic models for two-hour atmospheric seeing forecasts at Maunakea; LSTM and a normalizing-flow model (FloTS) give the best accuracy and calibrated uncertainty.
- **[COMPARATIVE EVALUATION OF MACHINE LEARNING AND DEEP LEARNING MODELS FOR WEATHER PREDICTION IN NIGERIA USING SPATIAL ANALYSIS](https://doi.org/10.70382/caijmasr.v13i9.056)**
  Compares Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM, Bi-LSTM and Transformer on 270,285 Nigerian station records; Transformer gives 19% lower RMSE than XGBoost for rainfall, temperature and humidity.

## Sub-seasonal to seasonal (1)

- **[Leveraging Machine Learning and Climate Phase Analysis to Enhance Seasonal Forecasts of Cold Extremes](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/aeb8ea)**
  Season-ahead ML models for Texas extreme cold days, conditioned on climate-phase states of three major modes, improve hindcast skill over single-phase models.

## Climate emulation (1)

- **[Sparse Gaussian-process emulation of regional climate projections with active budget allocation: a computer-science evaluation of tail-quantile fidelity in adaptation screening](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23606751)**
  Sparse Gaussian-process emulator with active budget allocation improves tail-quantile fidelity for regional climate projections at Lagos, with a (1-1/e) approximation guarantee; largely theoretical.

## Hybrid physics-ML & parameterization (2)

- **[From Physics-Based Parameterization to Data-Driven Emulation of Planetary Boundary Layer Turbulence in BAM- 1D](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10620663/v1)**
  Replaces the physics-based planetary boundary layer turbulence scheme in the BAM-1D model with a data-driven ML emulator. Abstract is missing, so details are limited.
- **[Physics-anchored residual quantum neural networks for cloud-cover parameterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26279)**
  Residual quantum neural networks for cloud-cover parameterization cut test MSE by 39-54% versus direct QNN prediction, but stronger classical models still outperform them.

## Data assimilation (3)

- ⭐ **[Batched NN 4D-Var: a performant hybrid model framework for the Integrated Forecasting System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32582)** · ECMWF
  Batched online learning of neural model-error corrections within ECMWF's IFS 4D-Var improves out-of-sample hybrid forecast skill, where sequential training did not generalize.
- **[Covariance-Informed Ensemble Score Filtering: Embedding Localized Covariance Geometry into Score-Based Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32148)** · Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Covariance-shaped forward diffusion embeds localized error covariance into score-based ensemble filtering, outperforming LETKF under strongly nonlinear observations in Lorenz-96 and SQG tests.
- **[Data‐driven emulation of flow‐dependent background‐error variance in variational data assimilation](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.70331)** · ECMWF
  ML emulates 50-member ECMWF EDA background-error variances from 5-member ensembles and runs in operational cycling, matching full-ensemble analysis impact.

## Downscaling & super-resolution (2)

- **[Physics-informed neural networks for downscaling convective-permitting rainfall extremes under sparse radiosonde initialization: a climate-analytics evaluation of tail-index distortion in dam-safety analytics](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23606783)**
  Proposes a tail-index distortion metric for auditing PINN rainfall-extreme downscaling under sparse radiosonde initialization; illustrated with Lagos temperature data, with no model validation.
- **[A Downscaling–Calibration Framework for GPM Precipitation Data Based on Geographically Weighted Machine Learning: A Case Study of the Yellow River Basin, China](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18203597)**
  Geographically weighted XGBoost downscales GPM precipitation from 0.1° to 1 km over the Yellow River Basin, and gauge-based calibration improves all products.

## Post-processing & calibration (2)

- **[TC-ANet 1: A temporally joint model for post-processing ensemble weather forecasts for in-situ locations and multiple target variables](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-3490)**
  Combines normalizing spline flows and flow matching to jointly post-process temperature, precipitation and gusts at in-situ sites on EUPPBench, rivaling state-of-the-art methods.
- **[Declining Surface Solar Radiation Resources in Polar Regions Under Global Warming](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125763)** · China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  U-Net corrects surface solar radiation from 12 CMIP6 models against ERA5, cutting mean biases by up to 91% and projecting polar declines under warming.

## Extremes & hazards (5)

- **[Pangu-Weather underestimates the 2019 European summer heatwave: A storyline comparison with ICON](https://doi.org/10.1175/jcli-d-26-0093.1)** · Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)
  Pangu-Weather underestimates the 2019 European heatwave, with a 2–3 °C cold bias and failure at extreme 2-m temperatures above 45 °C under +3 and +4 K warming.
- **[Future Changes in Early-Season Severe Weather: A Comparison of Machine Learning and Convection-Permitting Dynamical Downscaling](https://doi.org/10.1175/aies-d-25-0122.1)** · Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory / NERSC
  A U-Net trained on convection-permitting WRF output projects a 59% rise in early-season severe weather, versus 173% in the dynamical model, suggesting ML as cheap post-processing.
- **[Detection of East Asian Atmospheric Rivers Based on Physically Constrained Dual‐Threshold Methods and Deep Learning Algorithms](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026jd047067)**
  Physics-constrained dual-threshold algorithm builds a 1996–2023 East Asian atmospheric river dataset from ERA5, raising segmentation IoU from about 40% to over 80%.
- **[Large Projected Increase in Cloud-to-Ground Lightning Under Future Climate](https://doi.org/10.22541/essoar.15010629/v1)** · Technical University of Munich (TUM)
  XGBoost model driven by surface meteorology projects 76–140% more cloud-to-ground lightning under SSP3-7.0, with the largest relative increases at high latitudes.
- **[Accelerating Wildfire Hazard Mapping Across Canada with an AI Surrogate Modelling Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30170)** · Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC)
  Multi-task deep surrogate emulates BurnP3+ wildfire burn probability and intensity across Canada, giving 806-fold CPU speedup while preserving hazard classifications.

## Observations & remote sensing (2)

- **[Vision Transformers for Estimating Irradiance Using Data Scarce Sky Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30940)**
  Dual-branch vision transformers estimate diffuse horizontal irradiance from fisheye sky images in data-scarce settings, within 1-6 W/m² of a full-data ViT.
- **[UDL-RT: A unified differentiable all-sky microwave radiative transfer model with learnable parameterizations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24627)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  Differentiable PyTorch microwave radiative transfer model with NN gas-absorption and snow scattering parameterizations; cuts water-vapour brightness-temperature error spread 60-85% versus linear regression.

## Climate analysis & attribution (3)

- **[Evaluation of vegetation above-ground biomass carbon from CMIP6 ESMs using global satellite-based estimates](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/aeb7c9)**
  Evaluates 30 CMIP6 Earth system models' forest carbon against ESA CCI biomass, finding a 14% ensemble overestimate; explainable ML links errors to canopy height and forest age.
- **[Disentangling forced and internal components in observed climate evolution using spatiotemporal deep learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28219)**
  A ConvLSTM separates forced response from internal variability in observed climate, outperforming existing methods and attributing recent trends such as La Nina-like warming to internal variability.
- **[Interpretable Machine Learning Reveals Global Nonlinear Responses of Soil Respiration to Climate](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl124051)**
  SHAP-interpreted random forest on 8,111 observations reveals peaked temperature and saturating precipitation responses of soil respiration, suggesting Earth system models miss nonlinearities.

## Explainability, uncertainty & physics (1)

- **[Certified OOD Detection for Artificial Intelligence Climate Emulators Under Unseen Forcing Scenarios: Score Bounds and Seasonal Forecast Safety](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23545447)**
  Certified out-of-distribution detector for neural climate emulators using a latent-space score and conformal calibration, with bounds on silent acceptance under unseen forcings.

## Benchmarks, datasets & evaluation (3)

- ⭐ **[NNJA—a curated archive of Earth system observations from 1970s to present for reanalysis and data-driven model development](https://doi.org/10.1175/bams-d-25-0157.1)** · NASA, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), NSF NCAR / UCAR, Brightband
  Presents NNJA, a curated 1979-present archive of satellite, conventional and other Earth observations for reanalysis, plus an AI-ready Parquet companion dataset (NNJA-AI).
- **[TerraNostra-Bench: Benchmarking Spatial Downscaling and Temporal Rollout for Global 1-km Land-Use Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27793)**
  Introduces TerraNostra-Bench, a change-aware benchmark for 1-km annual land-use maps, showing autoregressive U-Net and Swin rollouts lose 5.7-7.2 accuracy points over 13 years.
- **[A Simple Climatology Benchmark Outperforms Machine-Learning Lightning Parameterizations](https://doi.org/10.22541/essoar.15010630/v1)** · Technical University of Munich (TUM)
  A training-mean climatology reproduces 92% of daily LIS/OTD lightning variance, beating ML lightning parameterizations and showing that their reported skill can be overstated.

## Applications & impacts (6)

- **[Projected Global Mortality and Economic Burden from Wildfire Smoke under Climate Change](https://doi.org/10.1021/ehp.6c00085)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  Deep learning wildfire-smoke PM2.5 and ozone fields drive projected mortality to 2100; SSP3-7.0 gives 5.52 million deaths, while SSP1-2.6 prevents 2.24 million.
- **[Interpretable Machine-Learning Warning of Tropical-Cyclone-Induced Port Service Disruption Using Automatic Identification System and Forecast Advisories](https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981261491700)**
  Interpretable logistic model warns of tropical-cyclone-induced US port service disruption from AIS and NHC advisories, reaching AUROC 0.70 on six events.
- **[Divergent responses of cropland GPP and SIF to compound drought and heatwave across China's climatic zones](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2026.115711)**
  ML with SHAP analysis shows compound drought-heatwave events cut cropland GPP by 14% across China, with climatic-zone-specific responses in GPP and SIF.
- **[Multi-site Learning for Forecasting Day-Ahead Wind-Power Production for Onshore Wind Farms in Great Britain with Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25370)**
  Multi-site bidirectional RNN for Great Britain day-ahead wind power forecasts cuts MAE 17.94% relative to NESO using NWP weather trajectories across 88 wind farms.
- **[Integrating Agrometeorology, Agronomy and Artificial Intelligence for Climate-Resilient Sustainable Food Production: A Critical Narrative Review](https://doi.org/10.9734/jeai/2026/v48i114583)**
  Narrative review of integrating agrometeorology, agronomy and AI, arguing ML weather forecasts and bias correction should be paired with process crop models for farm decisions.
- **[Leveraging satellite products and machine learning/deep learning models for the upgrading of agro-advisory systems in India: A bibliometric and systematic narrative review](https://doi.org/10.14719/pst.16505)**
  PRISMA review of 125 studies on satellite products and ML for Indian agro-advisories, arguing for AWS and satellite integration via downscaling and WRFDA assimilation.
