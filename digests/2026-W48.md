# 🌦️ ML4WX weekly digest · 2026-W48

**44 new papers** · **4 highlights** · 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-23

This week's papers cover three main areas: observation-driven and foundation-model forecasting, tropical cyclone and precipitation prediction, and climate emulators and downscaling for impacts such as heatwaves and solar power. Several papers also test what these models learn, including a sparse-autoencoder audit of GraphCast, an OSSE comparison of four AI models, and a benchmark showing conformal intervals can hit nominal coverage while hiding large day-to-day dispersion. The most useful papers to read are WeatherDreamer, which builds a world model that learns directly from multimodal observations without reanalysis and beats GEFS on near-term station ensembles. The second is the GPROF V08 pair, which describes the operational switch from Bayesian retrieval to a CNN trained on synthetic data produced by Satformer, so it is a rare case of ML going into a production satellite precipitation product. Readers working on forecast uncertainty should also look at the conformal prediction benchmark from ECMWF.

## ⭐ Highlights

**[GPROF V08: Improving Satellite Precipitation Estimates Across a Heterogeneous Constellation of Passive-Microwave Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19978)** · NASA, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
Operational GPROF V08 replaces Bayesian retrieval with CNN across the GPM constellation; a notable operational ML deployment for satellite precipitation.

**[WeatherDreamer: Learning a World Model from Multimodal Earth Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18393)** · Google DeepMind, Google Research, ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
End-to-end observation-driven world model from 18 observing systems without reanalysis, beating GEFS at short range; major step toward replacing data assimilation.

**[Enabling joint chemical and meteorological data assimilation with an atmospheric foundation model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24020)** · NVIDIA, ECMWF, University of Washington
Fine-tunes Aurora for joint chemistry-meteorology simulation and idealized DA for OH; promising direction for coupled chemical data assimilation.

**[FASTMIP-CMIP7: A Coordinated Multi-Emulator Ensemble for Rapid, Spatially Resolved Climate Projections under CMIP7 Scenarios](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6328)** · Met Office, ETH Zurich, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), University of Washington
Coordinated seven-emulator ensemble providing rapid CMIP7 scenario projections; valuable community resource for emulator-based climate projections and uncertainty assessment.


## Nowcasting (2)

- **[HM-SimVP: Hyperedge Interaction and Intensity Refinement for Heavy Precipitation Nowcasting](https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos17111129)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  HM-SimVP adds hypergraph interaction and gated intensity refinement to SimVPv2, raising CSI at 20 mm/h by 30% on CMPAS for heavy rainfall nowcasting.
- **[ForeSay: Tell the Future in Words, Branch the Details in Noise for Precipitation Nowcasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20815)**
  ForeSay uses a vision-language scenario prior and rectified-flow branching to cut ensemble nowcast cost from 8x to 5.2x while improving heavy-rain CSI by 10-84%.

## Global medium-range NWP (1)

- **[Interpretable Prototype-Conditioned Adaptation for Weather Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25848)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  ProtoCast uses group-specific prototype banks and low-rank operators to give interpretable local corrections, improving global and typhoon forecast accuracy.

## Probabilistic & ensemble forecasting (3)

- **[Resolution-adaptive generative ensemble prediction with integrated global-to-regional modelling on unstructured meshes](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-026-01579-2)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  HiRe-EFM is a generative ensemble model on unstructured global-to-regional meshes that improves global CRPS by 12.8% and high-resolution-area CRPS by 16.1%.
- **[Reinforcement-Aligned Probabilistic Weather Forecasting with Discrete Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25570)** · Tsinghua University (NowcastNet) · [code](https://github.com/Jinhan-Li/WeatherCraft)
  Discrete-token transformer generates ensembles and uses GRPO reward alignment; cuts day-15 RMSE 17.6% and runs about 18 times faster than GenCast.
- **[Run it Back: Autoregression vs. Video Diffusion for Final-Conditioned Weather Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21521)**
  Rewind-AR, a backward probabilistic autoregressive model on FourCastNet 3, generates final-conditioned extreme-event ensembles that better match ERA5 than cBottle-video, though up to 22% of members become unstable.

## Regional & km-scale models (1)

- **[Artificial Intelligence Applications in Earth System Modeling and Geoscience: A Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Precipitation and Soil Moisture Prediction](https://doi.org/10.59075/tamsaal.v4i11.280)**
  A CNN-LSTM with a physics-informed loss jointly predicts 24-hour precipitation and root-zone soil moisture over a mid-latitude region, cutting RMSE 18-24% versus benchmarks.

## Local & station forecasting (4)

- **[Optimized Deep Learning Ensembles for Urban Air Temperature Forecasting Using SLSQP](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42979-026-05441-3)**
  Optimises deep learning ensembles with SLSQP weighting for urban air temperature forecasting; the abstract is missing, so quantitative results are unavailable.
- **[Explainable Machine Learning for Rare Fog Prediction at Ivato Airport](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24362147)**
  XGBoost on METAR observations predicts airport fog three hours ahead with CSI 0.17 against 0.09 for persistence; SHAP shows pre-dawn humidity drivers.
- **[DTCN-RevIN: A Densely Temporal Causal Network with reversible instance normalization for enhanced weather forecasting via multi-scale feature integration](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0362741)**
  Causal dilated convolutions with RevIN forecast station weather over 32-to-6 steps; performance is comparable to TCN, with lower variance.
- **[A plug-in energy-balance freezing module for transformer frost nowcasting without leaf-sensor inputs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20114)**
  A transformer module embedding leaf energy-balance physics improves hourly frost-probability forecasts at 19 Korean stations without leaf-sensor inputs, beating LightGBM at all 12 leads.

## Sub-seasonal to seasonal (2)

- **[SeasonRainBench: Global Seasonal Precipitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22281)** · Tsinghua University (NowcastNet)
  SeasonRainBench is a global 1-5 month precipitation benchmark with 310,944 simulated months, plus a simulation-trained model improving CRPS by 3.6% over operational systems.
- **[Tracing Uncertainties of Extreme El Niño Prediction to Mesoscale Ocean Currents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17854)**
  Using CNOP perturbations on a differentiable ocean AI model, mesoscale ocean-current errors are shown to seed extreme El Niño forecast failures and align with the spring predictability barrier.

## Climate emulation (1)

- ⭐ **[FASTMIP-CMIP7: A Coordinated Multi-Emulator Ensemble for Rapid, Spatially Resolved Climate Projections under CMIP7 Scenarios](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6328)** · Met Office, ETH Zurich, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), University of Washington
  FASTMIP-CMIP7 provides a coordinated ensemble of seven emulators projecting CMIP7 scenarios, showing 2100 land warming of 2.3 to 3.9 °C and emissions as the main uncertainty.

## Data assimilation (3)

- ⭐ **[Enabling joint chemical and meteorological data assimilation with an atmospheric foundation model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24020)** · NVIDIA, ECMWF, University of Washington
  AuroraChem fine-tunes Aurora to jointly simulate meteorology and OH-relevant chemistry, showing chemical and meteorological observations can constrain each other in idealized data assimilation.
- **[An AI-Based OSSE Framework to Evaluate Learned Physical Consistency: A Multi-Model Study Using FourCastNet, Aurora, Prithvi WxC, and GraphCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22024)**
  First AI-based OSSE framework comparing FourCastNet, GraphCast, Aurora and Prithvi WxC response to ERA5 observation insertion, finding foundation models give the strongest impact.
- ⭐ **[WeatherDreamer: Learning a World Model from Multimodal Earth Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18393)** · Google DeepMind, Google Research, ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  WeatherDreamer learns an end-to-end observation-driven world model with a recursive DA-like cycle, beating GEFS on near-term station ensembles without reanalysis or gridded analyses.

## Downscaling & super-resolution (3)

- **[Multivariate deep-learning based regional climate model emulators and the impact of temporal awareness](https://doi.org/10.1175/aies-d-26-0008.1)** · Météo-France (incl. CNRM)
  Compares multivariate U-Net emulators of regional climate model temperature, finding a ConvLSTM temporal model outperforms the single-variable baseline across metrics.
- **[Urbanization Doubles Future Compound Heatwave Risk in the Yangtze River Delta Urban Agglomeration Based on a Machine Learning‐Statistical Hybrid Framework](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl123497)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)
  A stacking-ensemble ML and quantile-mapping framework downscales CMIP6 to 5 km for Yangtze River Delta compound heatwaves, reducing RMSE by 18% and projecting about four events per year by 2050.
- **[A physically constrained framework for reconstructing high-resolution solar irradiance from accumulated radiation in machine-learning weather forecasts](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6594)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  SolarFINE downscales accumulated AIFS and ERA5 radiation to 0.05 degree, 10-min irradiance with conditional diffusion, cutting hourly RMSE 29.9%.

## Post-processing & calibration (4)

- **[Observation-Free Representation Learning for Aerodrome Forecasting: The STATTCast and STATTTAF Pipeline](https://doi.org/10.22541/essoar.15012534/v1)** · Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC)
  An observation-free two-stage transformer pipeline converts convection-allowing HRDPS columns into aviation surface hazard forecasts, aiming to beat LAMP beyond six hours.
- **[Forecasting precipitable water vapor at unmonitored coastal sites using a sparse GNSS network and NWP forecasts](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11322350/v1)**
  Graph attention couples sparse GNSS precipitable water vapour stations with NWP to calibrate unmonitored coastal sites, cutting 1-24 h NWP error by 12.8% in China and 6.1% in the US.
- **[TRACE: Spatial Transferability of Explainable Machine-Learning Bias Correction of ERA5-Land Daily Temperature and Precipitation from Station-Rich Lowlands to High-Mountain Terrain](https://doi.org/10.1175/jhm-d-26-0122.1)**
  Explainable, conformal-calibrated gradient-boosting correction of ERA5-Land temperature and precipitation over Türkiye; transfer to high mountains retains 23% temperature error reduction.
- **[CorrectCast: Selective Multimodal Context for Scale-Aware Residual Correction in Precipitation Nowcasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22178)**
  CorrectCast adds a lightweight frozen-backbone residual corrector using QPE and PWV, raising mean CSI by 0.023-0.029 across six precipitation nowcasters.

## Extremes & hazards (5)

- **[Improving extreme rainfall forecasts over the Western Ghats through terrain-aware WRF ensembles and machine learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06743-5)**
  Combines terrain-aware WRF ensembles with machine learning to improve extreme rainfall forecasts over the Western Ghats; the abstract is missing, so details are limited.
- **[Evaluation of machine-learning and deep-learning SPEI drought forecasts against persistence across timescales and lead times in Bangladesh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25075)** · [code](https://github.com/mahirahnaf1727/bangladesh-spei-drought-forecasting)
  Across Bangladesh, ML drought forecasts at SPEI-6 beat persistence at 1-3 month leads, but skill and drought detection often disagree, so persistence-based evaluation is recommended.
- **[Comparing Data-Driven and Numerical Weather Prediction Forecasts of Extreme Weather: A Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23863)**
  Reviewing peer-reviewed comparisons, data-driven models generally beat NWP on temperature extremes and cyclone tracks but underpredict records, with mixed results for extreme precipitation.
- **[Quantifying the added value of human forecaster discussions for tropical cyclone intensity prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21602)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  Deep learning on NHC forecaster discussion text plus SHIPS predictors cuts 24-hour tropical cyclone wind MAE from 9.89 to 8.90 kt.
- **[Improving Tropical Cyclone Forecasts through Objective Ensemble Track-Error Reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20251)** · ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Met Office
  Comparing six objective ensemble-trimming and ML methods on 138 tropical cyclones, dynamic trimming reduces track error 8-16%, while gradient boosting gives the largest medium-range gain.

## Observations & remote sensing (5)

- **[Profiling tropospheric water vapor over China by combining ground-based GNSS meteorology and FY-4B/GIIRS data: A deep learning fusion approach](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosres.2026.109451)**
  Deep-learning fusion of ground GNSS meteorology and FY-4B GIIRS data to profile tropospheric water vapour over China; abstract unavailable, judged from title.
- **[End-to-End Landsat Land Surface Temperature Reconstruction with AlphaEarth Embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24243)** · NVIDIA
  U-Net fuses annual temperature cycle, coarse model LST and AlphaEarth embeddings to fill cloud gaps in Landsat LST, reaching 0.78 K MAE.
- ⭐ **[GPROF V08: Improving Satellite Precipitation Estimates Across a Heterogeneous Constellation of Passive-Microwave Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19978)** · NASA, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Operational GPROF V08 replaces Bayesian precipitation retrieval with a CNN fine-tuned on synthetic data, improving accuracy across GPM passive microwave sensors.
- **[Towards GPROF V08: A Machine-Learning Framework for Simulating Passive Microwave Observations of Precipitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19966)** · NASA, Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Satformer translates passive microwave observations between GPM sensors to generate synthetic training data, improving CNN precipitation retrievals for the full GPM constellation.
- **[Automatic Precipitation Level Classification of Satellite Cloud Images via Clustering and ResNet50](https://doi.org/10.1051/itmconf/20269302003/pdf)**
  ResNet50 with K-means pseudo-labelling classifies precipitation levels from satellite cloud images without manual annotation, offering a lightweight tool for grassroots observation.

## Climate analysis & attribution (1)

- **[The Dynamics of the Southern African Droughts Under ENSO and IOD Impacts in ERA5 and CMIP6](https://doi.org/10.1002/joc.70663)**
  Shows El Niño and positive IOD jointly intensify Southern African droughts, and CMIP6 projects a 35% rise in severe hydrological drought under SSP5-8.5.

## Explainability, uncertainty & physics (2)

- **[Auditing Learned Dynamics in Weather Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25119)** · Google DeepMind
  Sparse autoencoder features in GraphCast show causal use; amplifying convection features cuts tropical cyclone intensity error by a median 51%.
- **[Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification for Weather Forecasting: Conformal Prediction Under Spatiotemporal Dependence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24329)** · ECMWF · [code](https://github.com/Mariyaaleem/conformal-wx)
  Benchmarks conformal intervals for gridded 2 m temperature; near-nominal marginal coverage hides 3 to 10 times excess day-to-day dispersion of covered cells.

## Applications & impacts (6)

- **[China’s carbon neutrality strengthens its photovoltaic potential: Co-benefits for climate action and energy security](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aec4f2)** · Tsinghua University (NowcastNet), Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  Deep-learning 0.1° downscaling of Earth system projections shows carbon neutrality slightly strengthens China's PV potential (+0.27%) while high emissions reduce it (−0.49%).
- **[Predictability of solar irradiance declines with forecast horizon and limits hourly forecasting yet preserves monthly scale photovoltaic energy estimates for industrial rooftop planning in Bangladesh](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43937-026-00222-8)**
  Compares ten forecasting methods for Bangladesh solar irradiance, finding skill falls to near climatology beyond 24 hours, though 30-day PV energy block estimates remain usable (8% NRMSE).
- **[A data-driven machine learning approach to typhoon evacuation decision-making and system reliability evaluation of stochastic evacuation networks](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10479-026-07484-4)**
  Random Forest classifier on meteorological data predicts typhoon evacuation necessity, combined with stochastic network reliability analysis for Typhoon Gaemi in Taiwan.
- **[Explainable AI for Solar PV Power Forecasting: From Model Selection to Trustworthy, Reproducible Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25894)** · ECMWF, NASA
  Reproducible ML and explainable-AI workflow for one-day-ahead PV power forecasts from a 4 km NWP grid; best nRMSE is 11.6%.
- **[Temporally partitioned wind power forecasting with validation-calibrated residual diagnostics for PHM-oriented interpretation](https://doi.org/10.3389/fenrg.2026.1948217)**
  Benchmarks XGBoost, LSTM, GRU, TCN and PatchTST on synthetic wind-power data with day-ahead NWP covariates; XGBoost gives the lowest test MAE of 0.0657.
- **[Artificial Intelligence In Solar Energy Forecasting and Management](https://doi.org/10.47392/irjaeh.2026.0747)**
  Reviews AI methods such as random forests, SVR, and LSTMs for solar power forecasting and energy management, stressing weather-forecast integration; no new quantitative results.

## LLMs & agents (1)

- **[Does a completed simulation run mean a usable urban wind result for AI agents?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19385)**
  Introduces UrbanWind-AgentBench and WindPilot, showing LLM-run urban wind CFD needs raw-evidence checks; WindPilot raises mean task score from 62.5 to 72.5.
