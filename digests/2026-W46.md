# 🌦️ ML4WX weekly digest · 2026-W46

**45 new papers** · **2 highlights** · 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-09

This week's papers are mostly about making AI weather models cheaper and easier to understand, alongside a steady stream of work on tropical cyclones, solar and wind forecasting, and emulators. Two papers are worth reading first. "Half-precision inference of AI weather prediction models" shows that running Aurora, Pangu, FourCastNet, SFNO and FastNet in fp16 changes RMSE by at most 1.4% and speeds inference up by 1.3 to 3.5 times, a result that applies to most groups running these models. "Sonny-ENS" from ECMWF trains three MC-dropout networks on CRPS in 2.7 GPU-days, and the resulting ensemble beats ECMWF ENS on 96% of targets and GenCast on 69%, which questions how much compute probabilistic ML forecasting needs. Also of interest is the study of how much lossy compression ACE2 training data can tolerate, which finds that about 5x compression leaves predictions unaffected.

## ⭐ Highlights

**[Half-precision inference of AI weather prediction models: negligible loss of skill at a fraction of the cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09573)** · Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, Microsoft Research (AI for Science), ECMWF, Met Office, MeteoSwiss, The Alan Turing Institute · [code](https://github.com/sieste/ai-nwp-fp16)
Multi-centre study showing fp16 inference of five major AI weather models costs under 1.4% RMSE while giving up to 3.5x speedup and 3.7x energy savings; practical guidance.

**[Sonny-ENS: demystifying machine-learning ensemble weather forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01614)** · ECMWF
Cheap (2.7 GPU-days) MC-dropout CRPS ensemble beats ENS and mostly GenCast, with interpretability of spread; strongly lowers the barrier for ML ensembles.


## Global medium-range NWP (3)

- **[Leveraging transport theory to advance machine learning for weather forecasting](https://doi.org/10.14288/1.0457075)**
  Thesis proposes transport-aware neural architectures with semi-Lagrangian advection operators and transport-based generative methods for deterministic and probabilistic weather forecasting.
- **[Regional Evaluation of Pangu-Weather Driven by Multiple NWP Initial Fields over Shandong Province, China](https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos17111087)**
  Regional evaluation of Pangu-Weather initialized by ECMWF, CMA and NCEP fields finds the ECMWF-initialized version cuts 2 m temperature RMSE by 13.2% versus IFS.
- **[Low-Rank Clebsch--Gordan Coupling for Equivariant Spherical Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03411)** · NVIDIA, Princeton University
  Equivariant spherical neural operator with low-rank Clebsch-Gordan coupling cuts rotational error to numerical precision on WeatherBench and improves forecast skill over SFNO.

## Probabilistic & ensemble forecasting (2)

- ⭐ **[Sonny-ENS: demystifying machine-learning ensemble weather forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01614)** · ECMWF
  Sonny-ENS, three MC-dropout networks trained on CRPS in 2.7 GPU-days, beats ECMWF ENS on 96% of targets and GenCast on 69%.
- **[Physics-Informed Stochastic Neural Operators for Climate and Earth-System Prediction](https://doi.org/10.62311/nesx/rp2oct-30102026)**
  Proposes PISNO-Earth, a physics-informed stochastic neural operator for distributional climate prediction; it has a conceptual framework and a diffusion test case but no forecast skill results.

## Regional & km-scale models (1)

- **[Towards Rapid and Data-Efficient Urban Climate Assessment using Machine Learning Frameworks](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11241479/v1)** · Microsoft Research (AI for Science)
  Two ML frameworks emulate ADMS-Urban near-surface air temperature in Birmingham with R² up to 0.95, retaining accuracy with only 10% of training data.

## Local & station forecasting (4)

- **[A Digital Twin Framework Integrating Multiscale Signal Decomposition and Hybrid Deep Learning for Intelligent Water Storage Control on Remote Islands](https://doi.org/10.3390/su182111291)**
  Hybrid CNN-BiLSTM with signal decomposition forecasts 30-day daily precipitation on remote islands (MAE 2.49 mm, R2 0.83) for digital-twin rainwater control.
- **[Two-stage residual learning for calibrated uncertainty in solar irradiance forecasts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07444)**
  Two-stage LSTM and Gaussian process error model gives calibrated solar irradiance uncertainty, cutting standard-deviation mismatch by 56.5% versus the LSTM alone.
- **[Effectiveness and Limitations of Machine Learning for Predicting Lower Atmosphere Temperature Profiles](https://doi.org/10.1175/aies-d-26-0049.1)**
  LSTM models predict Dallas-Fort Worth morning lower-atmosphere temperature profiles from aircraft observations; best MSE is not best at capturing inversions, and extremes remain hard.
- **[RATSIFT: Resolution-Adaptive Transformer for Solar Irradiance Forecasting and Temporal Downscaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03738)** · Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Transformer forecasts and temporally downscales Dallas solar irradiance to 5-minute resolution with RMSE about 29-32 W/m2 and uncertainty intervals, using a genetic algorithm for tuning.

## Sub-seasonal to seasonal (1)

- **[Subseasonal prediction of tropical cyclone activity in the machine-learning model FuXi-S2S](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosres.2026.109427)** · Fudan University (FuXi), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  Evaluates subseasonal prediction of tropical cyclone activity from the FuXi-S2S machine-learning model; abstract not available, so assessment is based on title.

## Climate emulation (3)

- **[Stable probabilistic emulation of partially observed dynamical systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09317)** · New York University (M2LInES)
  Forced-dissipative stochastic interpolants give a stochastic climate emulator with proven geometric ergodicity and forcing stability, tested on two-timescale Lorenz-96.
- **[Loss of snow persistence during late season drives snow-season shortening in Northeast China under global warming](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-026-04156-w)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  Satellite-constrained 1-km ML emulators project Northeast China snow seasons shortening by 42–85 days by 2081–2100, using CMIP6 bias-corrected climate fields.
- **[Machine learning emulators of dynamic global vegetation models for fast gridded carbon budget predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04536)**
  CNN emulators of 19 dynamic vegetation models, driven by ERA5, reproduce global land carbon sink anomalies (median R2=0.81) on unseen years.

## Data assimilation (3)

- **[Machine Learning-Based Bias Correction Scheme for FY-4A AGRI Radiance Assimilation and Its Impact on Typhoon Forecasting](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18213810)** · China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  Kolmogorov-Arnold network bias correction of FY-4A water-vapour radiances beats variational bias correction, giving the smallest analysis errors and lowest track error for Typhoon In-Fa.
- **[A Survey of Nonlinear Filtering Methods for Data Assimilation and Recent Advances in Generative AI-Enabled Score Filters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07160)**
  Survey of nonlinear filtering for data assimilation, tracing the training-free Ensemble Score Filter and fourteen extensions for high-dimensional chaotic systems.
- **[Distinguishing Initial-Condition and Model Error Using Machine-Learning Weather Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05592)** · ECMWF, University of Washington
  Differentiable optimization of GraphCast initial conditions and LoRA weights, gated by Pangu-Weather, separates initial-condition from model error in reanalysis-shadowing forecasts.

## Downscaling & super-resolution (3)

- **[Comparative assessment of deep learning techniques for spatial downscaling of GPM IMERG satellite precipitation data for Jeju Island in South Korea](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jher.2026.100741)**
  Compares deep learning methods for spatially downscaling GPM IMERG satellite precipitation over Jeju Island, Korea; no abstract, so assessed from title only.
- **[A Super‐Resolution Model for Enhancing the Intensity and Structure of Tropical Cyclone Predictions](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026jh001372)** · China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  HybSR-TC uses a Swin Transformer to super-resolve Pangu-Weather forecasts to 2 km, improving tropical cyclone intensity and structure up to about 10 days lead.
- **[AI-Based Super-Resolution Approaches to Reconstruct Temperature Fields of Climatic Data Using Deep Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.3390/cli14110233)**
  ResNet, RNN and ConvRNN super-resolution upscales ERA5 2 m temperature from 1.0° to 0.25° with errors below 0.5 K and similarity above 0.97.

## Post-processing & calibration (1)

- **[Six-hourly Probabilistic Precipitation Forecasts Created From Hourly Probabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03326)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  A lightweight U-Net and GRU model turns hourly probabilistic precipitation into well-calibrated six-hourly Gamma-mixture forecasts out to 72 h, validated against MRMS.

## Extremes & hazards (2)

- **[Machine-Learning Classification of Next-Day Extreme Precipitation in Guangzhou](https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/2026.38487)**
  ConvLSTM detects 34 of 58 next-day extreme precipitation days in Guangzhou with highest recall and CSI, but produces 104 false alarms.
- **[A Data-Driven Tropical Cyclone Model Based on Lagrangian Limited-Area Transformers: Investigating Physical Fidelity through Real-Case Simulations and Idealized Experiments](https://doi.org/10.1175/jas-d-26-0056.1)**
  TC-centered Lagrangian limited-area transformer improves typhoon track prediction by about 30% over FourCastNetV2 and reproduces beta gyres and SST responses.

## Observations & remote sensing (5)

- **[Evaluation of a Multi-Link Machine Learning Approach for Rainfall Detection Using Satellite Microwave Links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09156)**
  Random forest and CNN classifiers on multi-link satellite microwave link data improve wet/dry rainfall detection over single-link approaches, with RF giving a more stable trade-off.
- **[Nowcasting of Tropical Cyclone Wind Fields and Intensity from Geostationary Imagery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08255)** · NVIDIA, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), University of Oxford, University of Cambridge (Aardvark)
  Simple neural networks retrieve tropical cyclone 2D surface wind fields and maximum-wind radius from geostationary imagery, improving rapid-intensification cases.
- **[Detecting Weather Fronts from Satellite Observations: What Does Reanalysis Add?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08148)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Met Office, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), KNMI (Royal Netherlands Meteorological Institute)
  ML detection of weather fronts from geostationary infrared reaches CSI 0.575 with ERA5 fields, versus 0.402 from satellite alone.
- **[A Deep-Learning Framework for the Generalized Emulation of Atmospheric Radiative Transfer at Microwave Frequencies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07094)**
  A fully connected network emulates microwave radiative transfer over 1-300 GHz, running more than 1000 times faster than CRTM with RMSE below 0.05 K.
- **[RadarFill: Observation-Constrained Gap Filling of Systematic Blind Zones in 3-D Weather Radar Reflectivity Mosaics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04768)** · Fudan University (FuXi), Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  RadarFill, an observation-constrained conditional diffusion model, fills low-level radar blind zones and improves downstream nowcasts at short lead times.

## Climate analysis & attribution (5)

- **[Projected changes in precipitation and temperature over the KKSD river basin using a machine-learning-based selective CMIP6 multimodel ensemble](https://doi.org/10.2166/wcc.2026.649)**
  Extra Trees selects a parsimonious four-GCM subset from bias-corrected NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 for the KKSD basin, projecting precipitation declines of 2.5–9.2% and Tmax rises up to 3°C.
- **[Three-way circulation–soil moisture–temperature feedbacks amplify the most extreme mid-latitudinal heat waves](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh9111)** · Columbia University (LEAP, Lamont-Doherty), Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK)
  Explainable ML shows a circulation–soil moisture–temperature feedback adds about 10% to extreme heat peaks and is underestimated in CMIP6 and CESM2 simulations.
- **[Dynamical Drivers of Interannual Variability in OMI Stratospheric NO 2 Revealed by Machine Learning](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026jd047752)** · NASA
  Gradient-boosted trees explain OMI stratospheric NO2 interannual variability better than regression, identifying tropopause pressure, QBO and ENSO as dominant dynamical drivers.
- **[Combined climate-mode regimes and South American precipitation anomalies: statistical inference and interpretable machine learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00382-026-08447-y)**
  Joint ENSO, PDO, SAM and Atlantic mode regimes are tested against South American rainfall; random forest and XGBoost out-of-fold skill is positive in the Amazon, peaking at R² 0.29 in DJF.
- **[A Prototype Probability Based Warning System for a Strong AMOC Decline](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-5755)**
  Probability-based early warning system for strong AMOC decline, tested with a stochastic box model and an Earth System Model with an ML update rule; shows Brier skill over persistence in an OSSE.

  Systematic review of 2018–2026 AI studies for climate prediction and environmental monitoring, highlighting energy efficiency and deployment gaps and proposing an energy-aware framework.

## Software, scaling & compression (3)

- ⭐ **[Half-precision inference of AI weather prediction models: negligible loss of skill at a fraction of the cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09573)** · Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, Microsoft Research (AI for Science), ECMWF, Met Office, MeteoSwiss, The Alan Turing Institute · [code](https://github.com/sieste/ai-nwp-fp16)
  Running AI weather models Aurora, Pangu, FourCastNet, SFNO and FastNet in fp16 with fp32 state changes RMSE by at most 1.4% and gives 1.3-3.5x speedup.
- **[How Much Lossy Compression of Training Data Can An Atmosphere Emulator Tolerate?](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6708)** · Allen Institute for AI (Ai2, ACE), NSF NCAR / UCAR
  Tests ZFP lossy compression of E3SMv3 training data for the ACE2 emulator, finding about 5x compression leaves predictions unaffected while 11x degrades metrics.
- **[Reproducibility Challenges and Provenance in a Multi-Machine Learning Workflow for Climate Downscaling](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23879132)**
  Examines reproducibility and provenance problems in a multi-machine-learning workflow for climate downscaling; no abstract, so assessed from title only.

## Applications & impacts (4)

- **[Multi-scale assessment of wavelet-guided deep learning for reference evapotranspiration forecasting to support vineyard water management](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agwat.2026.110892)**
  A CNN-LSTM forecasts daily reference evapotranspiration over 56 days in California's Madera County, with a 120-day input window giving the most stable skill, guided by a 93-day wavelet timescale.
- **[Robust Ranking of Port Impacts from Impending Tropical Cyclones: A Two-Stage Recommendation System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07296)**
  Two-stage recommender ranks Gulf of Mexico ports by tropical cyclone impact days; Wide & Deep models beat distance baselines by 55-86% in agreement.
- **[Mapping microclimatic spring frost risk through high-resolution spatial modeling in STYRIAN fruit- and winegrowing regions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cliser.2026.100757)**
  Stacked machine-learning regression kriging maps Styrian spring frost risk at 50 m from 151 stations, reaching R²=0.71 for climate-service site selection.
- **[Short-Term Forecasting of Heterogeneous Wind Power Fleets: Global Temporal Fusion Transformer Leveraging Static Covariates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06130)** · ECMWF, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD)
  Global temporal fusion transformer over 11 German wind farms, using ICON-D2 and ECMWF HRES inputs, beats local models at 87% of synthetic and 77% of real sites for 48-h power forecasts.

## LLMs & agents (3)

- **[Can Large Language Models Review the Scientific Literature? A Test Against a 102-Article Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05459)** · Google Research, University of Washington · [code](https://github.com/abx40/spanish-plume-llm-literature-review)
  Six AI tools tested on literature review of the Spanish plume weather pattern: all three full-corpus tools misclassified 40-79% of passing mentions as substantial.
- **[False Reassurance in LLM Severe-Weather Guidance: A Replayable Evaluation Against Real NWS Alerts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05290)**
  Replay of 117 NWS alert cases shows an LLM preparedness assistant gave false reassurance in 0% of cases with alerts versus 37.7% with a blind prompt.
- **[Can LLM Agents Improve Imperfect Earth System Model Parameterizations?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03543)** · Columbia University (LEAP, Lamont-Doherty)
  Tests ten language-model agents on writing differentiable warm-rain parameterizations; retuned physics matches most learned closures, while two agent closures slightly beat the best scheme.
