# 🌦️ ML4WX weekly digest · 2026-W45

**43 new papers** · **3 highlights** · 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-02

This week's papers focus on kilometre-scale regional forecasting, where several groups work on sharper precipitation and better extremes, and on bringing ML models closer to operational use. FitzRoy is worth reading for its use of reinforcement learning to refine a diffusion precipitation model, which gives realistic extremes at low latency and skill that matches or beats the state of the art up to 24 h over the US. HRRRCast is also worth reading because it checks a 3-km emulator against operational HRRR in seven months of near-real-time verification, which is rare evidence for how these models hold up in practice. Elsewhere, FASST shows a stable 140-year coupled run with an emulated atmosphere, and the AIFS-CRPS and MOGREPS-UK blending study shows that AI ensembles add value mainly through complementary errors.

## ⭐ Highlights

**[Skillful and realistic precipitation forecasting with reinforced diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38775)** · ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Met Office, ETH Zurich, Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
Reinforcement-learning-refined diffusion for US precipitation claims both skill and realism, with real-time deployment and flood case studies; the claims need independent checking.

**[FASST: A Compact Stateless Atmosphere Model for Long-Term Ocean Coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36297)** · NVIDIA
Stable 140-year coupled run with POP2 ocean and a stateless U-Net atmosphere emulator; ENSO amplitude is weak but it's a promising cheap coupled-climate approach.

**[Towards operational data-driven regional weather forecasting: extreme-event skill and near-real-time verification with HRRRCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36186)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
Year-long near-real-time verification of a 3-km data-driven regional emulator against operational HRRR, with native-resolution training and extreme-event loss gains; useful operational evidence.


## Nowcasting (1)

- **[STGAF-Net: A Multi-Source Spatiotemporal Gated Adaptive Fusion Network for Wind Speed Nowcasting](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18213692)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China Meteorological Administration (CMA)
  STGAF-Net fuses radar reflectivity and surface wind with gated spatiotemporal attention for 2-hour wind speed nowcasts, strongest at high-wind thresholds and later leads.

## Global medium-range NWP (2)

- **[Machine learning weather prediction at the operational threshold: architectures, data, evaluation, and the observation-interface frontier](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23788895)**
  Review of operational-stage ML weather prediction covering six architecture families, ERA5 dependence, a four-tier verification protocol, and end-to-end observation-based systems.
- **[Machine Learning Weather Prediction: Models, Verification and Limits](https://doi.org/10.63090/ijitrs/3139.3209.0040)**
  Review of ML weather prediction covering deterministic, probabilistic and hybrid models, precipitation nowcasting, benchmarks and critical failure studies, reporting no new experiments.

## Probabilistic & ensemble forecasting (1)

- ⭐ **[Skillful and realistic precipitation forecasting with reinforced diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38775)** · ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Met Office, ETH Zurich, Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  FitzRoy refines a conditional discrete diffusion precipitation model with reinforcement learning, matching or beating state-of-the-art skill up to 24 h over the US while producing realistic extremes and running in low-latency.

## Regional & km-scale models (2)

- **[Scale-aware and spatially-localised optimisation of kilometre-scale MLWP models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37325)** · Met Office, The Alan Turing Institute
  A dual-tree complex wavelet loss for kilometre-scale MLWP preserves multiscale structure and sharpens precipitation fields over the UK while keeping pointwise accuracy similar to MSE training.
- ⭐ **[Towards operational data-driven regional weather forecasting: extreme-event skill and near-real-time verification with HRRRCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36186)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  HRRRCast, a data-driven 3-km convection-allowing emulator, improves extreme-reflectivity skill via native-resolution training and weighted loss, and beats operational HRRR near-surface metrics in seven months of real-time verification.

## Local & station forecasting (5)

- **[A Machine Learning-Based Weather Forecasting System with Live Data Anchoring and Uncertainty Estimation](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23832326)** · [code](https://github.com/Yokiatch/weather-forecasting)
  XGBoost with live Open-Meteo anchoring produces recursive 7-day temperature forecasts with 95% intervals, cutting Day 1 RMSE from 2.45 to 1.12 for Greater Noida.
- **[QLFHNet: quantum long short-term memory forward harmonic network for weather forecasting utilizing time series data](https://doi.org/10.1080/02331888.2026.2744896)**
  Quantum-LSTM hybrid QLFHNet forecasts weather from time series with normalized RMSE 0.151 and R2 0.900, with limited evidence beyond benchmark numbers.
- **[RainCheck: Harnessing Historical Weather Data with Machine Learning for Rainfall Forecasting in Bangladesh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38211)**
  Leakage-aware evaluation of 618,650 Bangladesh station-days shows climatology rivals ML rainfall models, with lagged rainfall adding only modest next-day Brier skill of 0.074.
- **[Perbandingan Model Time Series ARIMAX, SARIMAX, dan Hybrid XGBOOST untuk Prediksi Suhu Harian di Kota Surabaya](https://doi.org/10.33005/senada.v6i1.552)**
  Compares ARIMAX, SARIMAX and a hybrid model for Surabaya daily mean temperature; the hybrid model gives the lowest MSE, RMSE and MAE.
- **[Perbandingan Algoritma Machine Learning dalam Prediksi Curah Hujan Balikpapan](https://doi.org/10.33005/senada.v6i1.512)**
  Compares five regression models on 2024 Balikpapan station data, finding Random Forest gives the most stable rainfall forecasts.

## Sub-seasonal to seasonal (2)

- **[Skillful subseasonal-to-seasonal ensemble forecasting with a multi-sphere coupled probabilistic model](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-026-01572-9)**
  TianXing-S2S uses a diffusion model with optimal-transport multi-sphere coupling to produce 45-day ensembles that beat ECMWF S2S and FuXi-S2S, and stays stable over 180-day rollouts.
- **[Seasonal Prediction of Summer Accumulated Cyclone Energy over the Western North Pacific Using Causality-Informed Predictor Selection and a Convolutional Neural Network](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11313293/v1)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  Causality-selected predictors feed CNN models that forecast summer western North Pacific accumulated cyclone energy with useful skill at 1–4 month leads.

## Climate emulation (2)

- **[Artificial Intelligence Applications in Climate Change Prediction](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23791670)**
  Review of AI for climate prediction spanning weather forecasting, multi-decadal climate simulation, sea level and emissions estimation, with hybrid physics-AI guidance.
- ⭐ **[FASST: A Compact Stateless Atmosphere Model for Long-Term Ocean Coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36297)** · NVIDIA
  FASST replaces the atmosphere and sea ice in a coupled climate model with U-Net emulators, achieving stable 140-year free runs with global SST close to CESM2 piControl.

## Downscaling & super-resolution (3)

- **[Operator-Aware Posterior Sampling for Multivariate Weather Downscaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39592)** · [code](https://github.com/CarloSaccardi/operator-aware-weather-downscaling)
  Learns the forward operator from paired data and normalizes guidance gradients per channel for diffusion-based downscaling, so one prior adapts across reanalysis sources and variables.
- **[High-resolution mapping of urban wet-bulb temperature and its intra-urban variability: A London case study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2026.105649)**
  Random-forest downscaling maps hourly 1-km wet-bulb temperature in London, finding peak wet-bulb occurs 0.7 h earlier than peak dry-bulb temperature.
- **[Climate downscaling with likelihood functional flow matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33482)**
  CD-FFM, a functional flow-matching downscaler, cuts CRPS of 1 km daily temperature over Fennoscandia by 12-18% versus a neural operator and sampling about 50 times faster than a CorrDiff-style model.

## Post-processing & calibration (5)

- **[Value of Adding AI Weather Model Ensembles to a Multi-model Probabilistic Blend](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42130)** · ECMWF, Met Office
  Blending the ensemble AIFS-CRPS with the Met Office MOGREPS-UK improves UK temperature and wind CRPS, with diversity and weighting analysis showing that complementary error structure drives the gain.
- **[Value of Aerosol Information for Day-Ahead Solar Irradiance Forecasting in a Dust-Prone Climate: A Multi-Site Evaluation of Machine-Learning Post-Processing in Iraq](https://doi.org/10.32870/2961bn58)**
  CAMS aerosol predictors cut dusty-hour clear-sky GHI and DNI RMSE by 4.5 to 10.6% in ML post-processing of ICON forecasts in Iraq.
- **[Day-ahead BOCNN-LSTM PV prediction based on input of NWP forecasting reconstruction by VMD and CNN-BiGRU-AM network](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rineng.2026.113747)**
  Two-stage VMD and CNN-BiGRU-attention NWP correction feeds a BO-CNN-LSTM day-ahead PV forecaster; corrected irradiance and temperature reach R² above 0.95.
- **[Teleconnection-aware machine learning calibration of multi-model seasonal forecasts for drought prediction over India](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00382-026-08419-2)**
  Teleconnection-aware machine learning calibrates multi-model seasonal forecasts to improve drought prediction over India.
- **[Dynamic conditional density network of wind vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35137)** · ECMWF, Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA)
  Neural-network-driven B-spline correction of bivariate normal ensemble wind densities improves log, energy and variogram scores at 154 Tohoku sites.

## Extremes & hazards (6)

- **[Improving extreme Fire Radiative Power forecasting with Gaussian loss-based transformer and LSTM models](https://doi.org/10.1080/01431161.2026.2749622)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  Gaussian-weighted Huber loss improves Transformer forecasts of extreme satellite fire radiative power, reducing underprediction of high-intensity fires while LSTM gains are weaker.
- **[Generated storm lifecycles fill the rare-event tail of short rainfall records](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41365)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  Generative model StormGen turns a storm's first hour into full hourly rainfall lifecycles, reproducing Hurricane Harvey's extreme totals for stress-testing flood protection in Texas.
- **[CYCLONE: a superfast large-scale coastal storm surge model for tropical cyclones – EGU26](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23761536)**
  Graph convolutional network predicts tropical-cyclone-induced coastal storm surge peaks across the North Atlantic in under one second, trained on ERA5-driven GTSM simulations.
- **[Characterisation and forecasting of heatwaves using HWMId: Change-point based time series intervention modelling with machine learning for Western Maharashtra, India](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06698-7)**
  Change-point intervention modelling combined with machine learning characterises and forecasts heatwaves in Western Maharashtra, India, using the HWMId index.
- **[Causal-prior-constrained deep learning for rapid-intensification warning of western North Pacific tropical cyclones](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40304)**
  CausalVisionRI adds a causal-graph prior to a deep model using satellite imagery for 24-h western North Pacific rapid-intensification warnings, reaching a Brier skill score of 0.259 on a 2020-2024 holdout.
- **[Progress toward forecasting excessive rainfall with random forests based on a deterministic convection-allowing model](https://doi.org/10.1175/aies-d-26-0005.1)** · NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Colorado State University (incl. CIRA)
  Random forests on HRRR convection-allowing forecasts improve excessive rainfall prediction with gridpoint aggregation, but trail the GEFS-based forest and the operational outlook.

## Observations & remote sensing (4)

- **[Tropical Cyclone Intensity Estimation from Full-Swath 183-GHz Microwave Satellite Observations](https://doi.org/10.1175/waf-d-26-0051.1)**
  Full-swath CNN D-MINT183v2 estimates tropical cyclone intensity from 183-GHz microwave imagery across sensors, extending usable microwave estimates beyond the inner swath.
- **[SATFRONT: Detecting Atmospheric Fronts with Satellite Data using Machine Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42111)** · ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC), Met Office, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD)
  SATFRONT detects cold, warm and occluded fronts over Europe from geostationary satellite data, reaching CSI 0.428 without reanalysis and 0.591 with ERA5, beating an operational rule-based detector.
- **[A two‑stage deep learning framework for multi‑source precipitation fusion to optimize seasonal, extreme and precipitation event estimation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejrh.2026.104139)**
  Two-stage UNet-SegNeXt fusion of six precipitation products yields daily 0.25° China rainfall with CC 0.817, outperforming ERA5 by 0.267 in correlation.
- **[Machine learning framework for the reconstruction of missing daily rainfall time series from multisource satellite and in situ observation in Burkina Faso](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40808-026-02949-8)**
  Hybrid classification-regression ML reconstructs missing daily rainfall in Burkina Faso from five satellite products, cutting annual median RMSE by about 2.5 versus most satellite datasets.

## Climate analysis & attribution (3)

- **[Global warming intensifies compound renewable energy droughts](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-026-04183-7)**
  CMIP6 projections show renewable energy droughts intensify with warming, affecting 69.4% of land at 3°C; machine learning identifies solar radiation as the dominant driver.
- **[Linear Versus Non‐linear Models in Predicting Low‐Cloud Regime Occurrences From Cloud‐Controlling Factors](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026jd047557)** · NASA
  Neural network outperforms ridge regression for monthly low-cloud regime occurrence, but global transfer needs extra training samples to cut errors by 30–50%.
- **[Climate‐Driven Straightening of Western North Pacific Tropical Cyclone Trajectories](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026ef008511)**
  Sinuosity-based ML classification shows western North Pacific tropical cyclone trajectories are straightening, driven by shorter duration and WPSH expansion, raising Northeast Asia landfall risk.

## Explainability, uncertainty & physics (2)

- **[Explaining patterns learned by neural networks for detection of atmospheric surface fronts in gridded atmospheric data](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-3877)** · Max Planck Institute for Meteorology
  Layer-wise relevance analysis of a U-Net front detector in ERA5 shows implausible patterns and tropical over-detection, so it should be used with care outside training regions.
- **[GNN-VIEW: Visual Inspection and Explanation of Graph-based Earth-System Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35693)** · ETH Zurich
  GNN-VIEW is an interactive browser tool for inspecting graph structure before training and attributing graph-based weather and ocean forecasts to input features, e.g. Hurricane Laura precipitation.

## Applications & impacts (4)

- **[Seasonal hydropower forecast framework using a combination of deep learning and physics-based methods](https://doi.org/10.31223/x50b98)**
  Hybrid pipeline chains seasonal forecasts, FNO downscaling, UMAL-LSTM runoff and routing to give probabilistic hydropower indicators, with CRPSS 0.25–0.33 at one-month lead.
- **[A weather integrated neural symbolic dispatch framework for power system operational security](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-73702-5)**
  Neuro-symbolic dispatch combining weather-driven forecast uncertainty with security constraints cuts operating cost 11.4–26.7% and constraint violations by over 80% on Danish DK1 data.
- **[IMPACT: Inverter-Informed Multimodal Learning for Photovoltaic Generation Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40418)**
  Physics-informed multimodal model with graph attention over inverter cloud dynamics gives 10 to 110 minute probabilistic PV forecasts, beating 14 baselines with skill score 0.295.
- **[Multistep and Multivariate Time Series Explainability with GradientSHAP: A Wind Power Case Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40330)**
  Adapts GradientSHAP to explain multistep multivariate wind power forecasts, offering local and global explanations of environmental drivers for day-ahead grid management.

## LLMs & agents (1)

- **[From extreme weather to agricultural consequences with agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40641)** · NASA
  An agent links weather hazard indices to crop loss in Florida, reproducing insurance losses within 2% and detecting cyclone days with AUC 0.98.
