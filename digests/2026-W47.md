# 🌦️ ML4WX weekly digest · 2026-W47

**27 new papers** · **3 highlights** · 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-16

This week's papers lean on coupling and reuse: AI weather models are used as components in larger systems, and several groups use generative or learned downscaling to produce regional climate information. Rescene-ESM from NVIDIA is the paper most worth reading, since it couples a frozen AI weather model to physical ocean, sea-ice, land and river components, runs about 1,000 simulated years per day and reproduces El Niño. Also worth your time is the NCAR study on ensemble covariance propagation, which shows GraphCast working inside a cycled EnVar/EnKF system with real observations and giving spread comparable to a physics-based reference. Other work this week covers precipitation estimation (PARDIG), downscaling of extremes and heatwaves, and tests for physical consistency in foundation models (PhysXAI), though a few entries had no abstract available.

## ⭐ Highlights

**[Rescene-ESM: an Earth system model built around a frozen AI weather model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11066)** · NVIDIA
Couples a frozen AI weather model to physical ocean, land and river components, giving a fast ESM with ENSO and about 1000 years per day on one GPU.

**[PARDIG: Global Precipitation Estimates Learned from Sparse Satellite Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13941)** · Google Research
Global 0.1°, 15-minute ML precipitation product trained on sparse radar, beating IMERG, MSWEP, ERA5 and HRES; likely a practically useful dataset.

**[Ensemble Covariance Propagation with Machine Learning Weather Prediction Models in a Realistic Data Assimilation System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11918)** · NSF NCAR / UCAR
Shows GraphCast can propagate EnKF covariances in a realistic cycled DA system with real observations, a useful step toward ML in operational ensemble DA.


## Nowcasting (1)

- **[Physics-constrained transformer for medium-to-low altitude wind speed and precipitation short-range forecasting](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.116680)**
  Physics-constrained transformer for short-range wind speed and precipitation forecasts at medium and low altitudes; abstract unavailable, so details are limited.

## Local & station forecasting (1)

- **[Probabilistic high-temporal resolution surface wind nowcasting with deep learning models](https://doi.org/10.1175/aies-d-26-0063.1)** · Bureau of Meteorology (Australia)
  Probabilistic deep learning wind nowcasts at individual sites for aviation reduce forecast error by about 10–30% versus hourly NWP, with a weighted ensemble adding gains.

## Sub-seasonal to seasonal (1)

- **[Extending weather forecasts from hours to weeks with teleconnection-aware graph neural networks](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11430274/v1)** · Technical University of Munich (TUM)
  Reuses a pretrained medium-range model with lightweight temporal attention and a teleconnection-index graph module to extend forecasts to four weeks, updating only 10.9% of parameters.

## Climate emulation (3)

- **[The calibration deficit: a challenge for Earth system simulation and emulation](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6437)** · California Institute of Technology (Caltech), University of Washington
  Argues Earth system models are poorly calibrated to historical warming and proposes hybrid process-based and ML/AI calibration as a route forward.
- ⭐ **[Rescene-ESM: an Earth system model built around a frozen AI weather model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11066)** · NVIDIA
  Rescene-ESM couples a frozen AI weather model to physical ocean, sea-ice, land and river components, running about 1,000 simulated years per day and reproducing El Niño.
- **[Guiding future global temperature distributions with simulations and observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10882)** · Columbia University (LEAP, Lamont-Doherty)
  A conditional diffusion emulator trained on 17 CMIP6 models and ERA5 uses guidance toward observations, halving bias in extreme temperature percentiles and enabling large ensembles.

## Data assimilation (2)

- ⭐ **[Ensemble Covariance Propagation with Machine Learning Weather Prediction Models in a Realistic Data Assimilation System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11918)** · NSF NCAR / UCAR
  Shows GraphCast can propagate ensemble covariances in a cycled EnVar/EnKF system assimilating real observations, with spread comparable to a fully physics-based reference.
- **[Analysis increments for model bias diagnosis and correction: Theoretical foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11162)** · University of Reading
  Theory shows what analysis-increment-based bias correction measures and proves iterating it removes all observable bias in a Kalman filter, with Lorenz '96 tests including a learned correction.

## Downscaling & super-resolution (4)

- **[Deep-learning downscaling of CMIP6 heatwave projections for Greece and comparison with a regional climate model](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6743)**
  Deep-learning downscaling of five CMIP6 models to 5 km projects Greek heatwaves rising from about one to seven per season under SSP5-8.5 by 2051–2080.
- **[Joint near-surface wind downscaling and hub-height estimation for wind power projections](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40537-026-01569-4)**
  A ResUNet downscales ERA5 winds to 0.25° at 10 m and 100 m, cutting hub-height speed RMSE by 24% versus the power-law extrapolation for wind power.
- **[Trade-offs in deep learning approaches for precipitation downscaling across weather regimes](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-80185-x)**
  Benchmarks CNN, U-Net and diffusion precipitation downscaling over Japan, finding trade-offs by metric and an asymmetric-loss U-Net most robust during typhoons.
- **[A hybrid machine learning-analog framework for downscaling unprecedented climate extremes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15284)** · Météo-France (incl. CNRM), ETH Zurich, Swiss Data Science Center (SDSC)
  Combines generative ML downscaling with analog sampling to produce high-resolution unprecedented heatwave events over Paris from coarse global climate model boosting runs.

## Post-processing & calibration (1)

- **[Monthly model-adaptive machine-learning ensemble of CMIP6 models for global land temperature and precipitation projections](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06716-8)**
  A model-adaptive machine-learning ensemble combines monthly CMIP6 outputs to project global land temperature and precipitation (abstract unavailable).

## Extremes & hazards (3)

- **[A Novel Deep Learning Approach for Tropical Cyclone Path Prediction Based on LSTM-BiLSTM](https://doi.org/10.5750/ijme.v168ia6(s).2062)**
  LSTM-BiLSTM forecasts tropical cyclone landing locations from track and meteorological features, reporting 97% accuracy on 2007-2017 data.
- **[Physics-informed and data-efficient machine learning for storm surge forecasting during typhoons](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh5673)** · Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST)
  Physics-informed neural network embeds shallow water equations to forecast typhoon storm surge, outperforming data-driven baselines beyond 16 hours with less training data.
- **[Physics-guided deep learning with spatial decoupling for tropical cyclone convective precipitation prediction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2026.129445)**
  Physics-guided deep learning with spatial decoupling predicts convective precipitation in tropical cyclones; abstract unavailable, so details are limited.

## Observations & remote sensing (4)

- **[Reconstructing catchment precipitation with deep learning and a large network of streamflow gauges](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-6840)**
  EA-LSTM reconstructs daily precipitation for 906 US catchments from streamflow and weather, reaching median KGE 0.771 with runoff included.
- **[HybridCT: ML Accelerated Tomography for Climate Relevant 3D Cloud Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16255)** · NASA, California Institute of Technology (Caltech)
  HybridCT uses an ML emulator with tomographic reconstruction to retrieve 3D low-cloud extinction from multi-angle radiances in 3.5 s, a more than 500-fold speedup over AT3D.
- ⭐ **[PARDIG: Global Precipitation Estimates Learned from Sparse Satellite Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13941)** · Google Research
  PARDIG learns global 0.1°, 15-minute precipitation from geostationary imagery and sparse spaceborne radar, beating IMERG, MSWEP, ERA5 and HRES, with a 1999-present historical variant.
- **[Reconstructing Boundary Layer Wind Profiles with Machine Learning: A Comparative Study on IGRA Radiosonde Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13359)** · ECMWF, NOAA (incl. GFDL, PSL, GSL, NCEP/EMC)
  Tabular learners cut boundary-layer wind RMSE from about 3 to 1.3-1.6 m/s on 158k radiosondes, with predictors, not architecture, setting accuracy.

## Climate analysis & attribution (2)

- **[Towards ML-Based Weather-Dependent Diagnostic Index for Urban Heat Island Intensity in the Arctic Cities](https://doi.org/10.3390/cli14110245)**
  CatBoost captures up to 64% of Arctic urban heat island variance from weather, and a universal index generalizes to unseen cities with average R² of 0.49.
- **[Exploration of the northern stratospheric polar vortex extremes via archetype analysis and tropospheric imprint](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11367588/v1)**
  Unsupervised archetype analysis identifies extreme stratospheric polar vortex states that drive NAO/AO tropospheric responses lagging four to five weeks, suggesting subseasonal predictability.

## Explainability, uncertainty & physics (1)

- **[PhysXAI: A Theoretical Framework for Diagnosing Physical Hallucinations in Weather Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15053)**
  PhysXAI learns to detect, localise and attribute physical violations such as mass and hydrostatic imbalance in GraphCast, Pangu-Weather and NeuralGCM forecasts.

## Benchmarks, datasets & evaluation (1)

- **[A Validation Framework for Machine Learning Emulation of Integrated Assessment Models](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24157785)**
  Proposes a multi-pronged validation framework for machine-learning emulators of integrated assessment models, grounded in philosophy of science and simulation validation theory.

## Applications & impacts (3)

- **[Advancing spatial hurricane storm surge risk assessment using machine learning and simulations under changing climate scenarios](https://doi.org/10.1080/10106049.2026.2756760)**
  Machine learning trained on insurance losses estimates hurricane storm-surge building damage under RCP scenarios, projecting Category 5 frequency rising 58-104%.
- **[A hybrid BAG–CARIMA–GPM model for climate-induced shifts in the solar power potential based on PV and CPV technologies](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nxener.2026.101195)**
  Hybrid bagging-ARIMA model bias-corrected CMIP6 projections to forecast Nigerian solar irradiance and PV output shifts under SSP scenarios.
- **[Perils and opportunities of AI for public climate services](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12106)**
  Uses PESTLE and expert consensus to assess governance, equity and sovereignty risks of AI-enabled public climate services in Europe; a policy perspective, not a model.
